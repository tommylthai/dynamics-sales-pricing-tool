Copilot Credit entitlement

0 included Copilot Credits per month

Only Sales Premium has a documented included entitlement: 1,000 Copilot Credits per licensed user/month.

  • Credits accrue at the tenant level and should be allocated to the Sales environment.
  • Monthly license-based credits are not cumulative and reset each month.
  • Included product Copilot features are distinct from metered Copilot Credits.

Estimate assumptions

  • USD public list pricing for the United States.
  • Per-user monthly equivalent with annual commitment.
  • Annual total equals 12 monthly periods.
  • Taxes, implementation, support, storage add-ons, consumption overages, and other products are excluded.
  • Custom mode reflects user-entered quote values and is not presented as Microsoft's list price.

Licensing cautions

  • Professional and Enterprise users may not be deployed in the same environment.
  • A full-access user's first Dynamics 365 application generally must be the highest-priced qualifying base license.
  • Attach licenses can be assigned only with an appropriate qualifying base license.
  • Relationship Sales pricing must be obtained from Microsoft and requires at least 10 seats.
  • Microsoft Product Terms and the current licensing guide control if this tool differs from them.
